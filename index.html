How to use Nexus

Feature guide and data-handling details.

1. Overview

Overview summarises active topics, topics due today, overdue topics, reviews completed today, progress by subject, and the nearest upcoming exam or deadline. “Start review” opens the next due topic.

2. Topics and spaced repetition

Use Add topic to create a lesson. Choose a stream and subject, enter the name, and add optional notes. Use the search box and filters to find topics by name, stream, subject, or due status. Edit an existing topic to rename it or move it. Delete removes the topic from the tracker but its older history entries remain.

Open Review after attempting to recall the material without looking. Choose a recall rating from 1 to 5 and optionally set a custom next review date. Check the preview, then tap Mark as reviewed to save the review. The next due date is based on the review date, rating, and previous interval unless you choose a custom date. Rating 1 resets to one day; higher ratings progressively increase the interval. The maximum interval is 120 days.

3. Streams and subjects

In Setup & data, add streams or courses, then add subjects to each stream. You can use any language and any subject. The starter Commerce stream includes Sinhala Business Studies, Accounting, and Economics lesson names; you can edit these or add another study area.

4. Exams and deadlines

Add any number of upcoming exams, assessments, or deadlines. The overview highlights the nearest future date. Past exams remain listed so you can retain a record; edit or delete entries when required.

5. Review history

Every rating adds a history record with a date, topic/subject snapshot, rating, interval, due date, and optional note. Search or filter the log and export it to CSV. Deleting history does not change the current topic schedule.

6. Backup and restore

Download backup (JSON) exports all app data. Keep a copy outside the browser. Restore validates a backup and replaces the current local data after confirmation. CSV exports are intended for spreadsheet review and are not full backups. Importing an older tracker backup is supported where its data format can be recognised.

7. Theme and installation

The theme button cycles through device setting, light, and dark. On a supported browser, choose Install to add Nexus to your home screen or desktop. If installation is not offered by the browser, open the browser menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen” where available.

8. Study Hub

Study Hub adds a budget-based daily plan, estimated topic mastery and weakness detection, configurable expected syllabus counts, a due calendar, an FSRS-inspired optional schedule heuristic, focus-session time logging, practice-paper records, a mistake journal, goals, analytics, bulk topic actions, CSV topic import, and expanded backups. Notifications run only while the app is open and permission is granted; closed-browser reminders require extra push infrastructure.

9. Privacy, storage, and limitations

Your data stays in the current browser's local storage unless you export it. It is not automatically synced to other devices, and clearing browser data can remove it. Use backups when changing devices. Do not treat a browser copy as your only record. The app can be installed and used offline after it has first been opened from a secure hosted address; offline changes remain on that device until you export them.
